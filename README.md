# Bike Shop Sales Dashboard

## Overview
A Power BI project analyzing bike shop sales performance from 2016 to 2018.

## Tools
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue
- Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Sales Overview**
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue by category and store

**2. Product & Sales Performance**
- Top 5 products by revenue
- Revenue distribution by brand
- Top 5 salespeople by revenue
- Leading product and brand

## Objective
Transform sales data into actionable insights to support business decision-making.
